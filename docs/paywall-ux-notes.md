# Paywall / onboarding UX notes

Running notes for a later "super design" pass on the paywall and
onboarding flow - not a spec, not scheduled to build yet. Collected
while researching real examples (YouTube UX breakdowns) of high-grade
onboarding/paywall design that minimizes friction and maximizes
conversion, for once the basic app + desktop functionality is fully
working and tested for real.

**Sequence, per direct instruction:** Function, emotion, payment.
Build and prove the function first (current work - pairing, sync,
conflict resolution all real and tested). Emotion and payment layer on
top of that once the foundation holds up on a real device, not before.

## Working concept: a mascot character (the dog)

Walks the user through onboarding/paywall moments with micro-rewards -
happy doggy animations, confetti, snappy blob-style transition effects -
the kind of small delight moments that make an onboarding flow feel
good to sit through, not just functional. Not designed yet - a
direction, not a spec.

## Small effective patterns to fold in later

Seed list - add to this as more turn up during research, rather than
losing them in chat:

- **Tick with text**, not a bare checkmark icon alone - pairing a ✓ with
  a short word/phrase reads as a completed benefit, not just a
  decoration.
- **Right arrow on buttons** - a directional cue that reinforces forward
  progress through a flow, not just a flat label.
- **Micro-rewards** - small positive reinforcement at real completion
  moments (finishing a step, a successful sync, resolving a conflict),
  not applied everywhere indiscriminately.
- **Character emotions and connection** - the mascot's expression/state
  reacting to what the user just did, building a relationship with the
  app rather than a purely transactional flow.
